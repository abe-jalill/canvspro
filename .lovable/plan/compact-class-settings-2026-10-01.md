# Compact Class Settings

## What will change
- Replace the separate “Class names” and “Which classes to show” cards with one compact **Class settings** row.
- Keep it collapsed by default so it uses very little space.
- When opened, show two small tabs: **Class names** and **Classes shown**.
- Preserve the existing rename, hide/show, loading, and save behavior unchanged.
- Rename the Settings side link from “Courses” to “Class settings” and keep announcements separate.

## Verification
- Check the collapsed and expanded layouts on desktop and phone sizes.
- Confirm both tabs switch correctly and all existing controls remain usable.
- Confirm the app builds without errors.
